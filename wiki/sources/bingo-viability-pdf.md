Demand is real (resilience, customization, digital inventory); blockers are "connecting tissue," not tech. Enter via industrial spares/MRO + low-risk custom consumer goods, NA/EU first. Graveyard: Shapeways bankruptcy mid-2024 (centralized bureaus don't scale, p.3); 3D Hubs killed its P2P maker tier in 2018 over QC variance (pp.3–4). Xometry pain points: opaque matching, no supplier contact (pp.3,6 — profitability claim outdated, see [[bingo-landscape-2026]]). Würth Digital Inventory Service on Cardano: tokenized designs, capped print counts, auto-royalties — per-print licensing works in B2B (pp.2,4,5). Stated open space: "no major e-commerce marketplace integrates on-demand distributed fabrication" (p.4). Franchise kit (approved machines/materials) proposed to close the quality gap (pp.5–6). Gaps: cross-platform manufacturer reputation, idle long-tail capacity, LatAm/Africa.
