Feasibility study: AI matching + blockchain settlement + IoT fabrication nodes is feasible today; innovation is synthesis, execution-gated; recommends narrow additive-first spare-parts pilot. Precedents: Festo/Fetch.ai per-process negotiating agents (pp.1–3); Xometry AI quoting (p.1,10); Project DIAMOnD — 500+ small manufacturers, 50k+ parts, auto payment splitting (pp.2,7); 3DOS royalties-anywhere (pp.1–2,10); Exeter patented print watermarking + licensing (pp.3,5); staking/reputation for QC (p.9). Loses to mass production on commodity volume (p.6). Risks: QC variance, IP leakage post-delivery, liquidity bootstrap, chain fees, agent-mispricing accountability, liability/KYC (pp.5–7). **Conflict:** its Web3-as-core framing is superseded by [[bingo-landscape-2026]].
