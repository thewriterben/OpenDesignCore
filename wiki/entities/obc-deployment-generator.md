Expo/React Native (iOS/Android/web) wizard: Inventory → Feature Desires → Review → complete deployment (topology, TOML config, site plan, downloadable compilable ESP32-S3 firmware). On-device, offline-first; consumes [[oh-ben-claw]] registry.json and planner via WASM. **The closest existing analogue to the design-platform front end** — the same 3-step shape generalizes to "what can I build with what I own."
