A low-cost, high-quality 3D scanner that you can build yourself - completely open source and modular. 3D scanning opens up completely new possibilities in many areas, such as 3D printing, modelling, reverse engineering, product presentations, photography and more.

*(before/after slider: giant_swallowtail_color.jpg → giant_swallowtail_render.jpg)*

**Unique 3D Scanning technique** — Using photogrammetry on small scale has never been more accessible. OpenScan utilizes this technique to create stunning 3D scans.

**No App needed** — We strongly believe in data autonomy and give users the choice to process their data locally or through our in-house [OpenScan Cloud](https://openscan.eu/pages/openscancloud) photogrammetry engine.

**Strong Community** — As a community-driven project, OpenScan is active on [many plattforms](https://openscan.eu/pages/about), where users actively participate in its development

**Modularity** — Every part of OpenScan is [well documented and highly modular](https://openscan.eu/pages/documentation), so that users can modify and build their own devices based on the existing solutions.

**Open Source** — Without the [open source movement](https://github.com/OpenScan-org), this project would not be possible. For this reason, all our firmware and hardware plans are published and ready to be modified.
